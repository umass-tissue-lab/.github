## Hi there 👋

Welcome to the **Buttenschön Lab** at the University of Massachusetts Amherst!

### 🧬 What We Do
We use mathematical modeling and computational biology to understand how cells organize into functional tissues. Our research bridges mathematics, computation, and biology to uncover universal principles governing:
- 🩹 Wound healing
- 🐣 Embryonic development  
- 🦠 Cancer metastasis

### 🔬 Our Approach
We develop and analyze:
- **Mathematical models** using dynamical systems, bifurcation theory, and nonlocal differential equations
- **Cell-based computational frameworks** to track individual cell behaviors and forces
- **Multiscale methods** connecting molecular to tissue-level phenomena
